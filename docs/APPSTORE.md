## Description
Carton tracks your packages and tells you one thing first: how many days until it shows up.

Paste a UPS tracking number, or the whole tracking link from an email, and Carton does the rest. It reads the status straight from UPS, works out the delivery date, and puts a countdown next to every package. Tap one for the last scan, the service and where it's headed.

There's no account to make. Carton has no servers, so there's nothing to sign into and nothing to leak. Your list lives on your phone and syncs to your other devices through iCloud.

When a package arrives, swipe it into the archive so the list stays short.

What's in it:
- A countdown to the delivery day for every package
- Paste a number or a full tracking link, and Carton spots tracking numbers on your clipboard
- Status, estimated delivery, last scan, service and destination
- Archive for delivered packages
- iCloud sync across your devices
- No account, no ads, no tracking of you

Carton supports UPS today. More carriers are coming.
